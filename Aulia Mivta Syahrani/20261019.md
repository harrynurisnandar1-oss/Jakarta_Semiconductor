Lesson Learned



Learned that the ZERA MT3000 features a configuration with an accuracy class of 0.02, making it potentially suitable for use as a reference meter for active energy testing of 0.5S and 0.2S class smart meters.



However, the ZERA MT3000 only supports harmonic measurement up to the 40th order, whereas the smart meter under development requires harmonic measurement up to the 50th order.



Therefore, this device does not yet meet the requirements for harmonic testing.
