Lesson Learned

The notation for measurement parameters follows the SPLN, as follows:
Active power = W
Reactive power = VAR
Apparent power = VA
Active energy = Wh
Reactive energy = varh
Apparent energy = Vah
Power factor = pf

The phase-to-phase RMS voltage is defined as the effective value of the electrical voltage measured between two phases in a 3-phase electrical system.
The R-S phase RMS voltage is calculated as the square root of the sum of the squared differences between the instantaneous voltages of phase R and phase S, divided by the total number of measurement samples.
