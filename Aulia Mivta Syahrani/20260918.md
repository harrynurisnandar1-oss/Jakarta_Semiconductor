Lessons Learned

Learned that UART and Low-Power UART (LPUART) support asynchronous communication, whereas USART supports both asynchronous and synchronous communication; Low-Power UART is used for low-power communication.

Understood the function of the X-NUCLEO-LPM01A for current measurement.

Learned that a multimeter is more suitable for current measurement under static conditions, while a current transformer is better suited for dynamic current measurement.

Understood the application of ThreadX on the STM32U575 to operate the system in low-power mode.

Understood the process of implementing ThreadX Low Power on different boards: the Nucleo U575Z1T6Q and the STM32U575VGT6.


Obstacles Encountered

I encountered difficulties while running the ThreadX Low Power on the Nucleo-U575Z1T6Q and STM32U575VGT6 due to unstable cable connections and a limited understanding of peripheral power consumption concepts on these boards.
