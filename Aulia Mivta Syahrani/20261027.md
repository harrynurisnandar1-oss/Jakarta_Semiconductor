This program is executed using an ADE9000 mock device.

Parameter values ​​for each phase are set to be identical, as this is currently in the simulation stage.

Reactive energy is simulated only for Q1, given that the active power (P) and reactive power (Q) values ​​currently being used are both positive.
In real-world conditions, reactive energy values ​​are not necessarily present in every quadrant.

At present, reactive energy is simulated only for Q1 because the parameters used represent a scenario involving the consumption of both active and reactive power, typical of inductive loads such as electric motors, air conditioning units, and transformers. Conversely, Q2, Q3, and Q4 depend on the direction of power flow and the specific characteristics of the load or user installation. For instance, reactive energy in Q2 is recorded when a solar panel system exports active power to the grid while simultaneously importing reactive power.
