## What I Did
- Identified firmware checks for W25Q128 compatibility (`nvram_storage.c` JEDEC ID: `EF 40 18` vs. `EF 40 17`, 16 MB capacity and address definitions).
- Replaced the Liu-Layland RMS schedulability analysis in the design document with **Extended Response Time Analysis (RTA)** while retaining the existing 8-task priorities, periods, and stack allocations.
- Standardized workflow descriptions for all 8 FreeRTOS task flowcharts based on the actual IPC (`xMeasMutex`, `xStorageQueue`) and watchdog (`PD12`, `0xEF`) implementation.

## What I Learned
- Low CPU utilization alone does not guarantee deadline compliance when FreeRTOS priorities are assigned based on functional criticality rather than task periods. Extended RTA accounts for higher-priority task interference and mutex blocking (`B_i`).
- SPI Flash compatibility requires checking driver capacity and address handling, not just matching the JEDEC ID.
