## 3. Comments, Questions, Complaints

### Comments
- The 0 Ω resistor between `3V3_MAIN` and `DVDD_3V3_IN` lets one PCB serve both the prototype and the production version. In production, the USB input, LDO, and power mux are not mounted, and the resistor supplies the MCU instead. It also makes debugging easier, because the MCU supply can be disconnected or the current measured at this point. The resistor and the power mux (U5) must never be mounted at the same time.
- Each footprint must follow the dimensions and land pattern of the actual component, otherwise the part cannot be soldered correctly on the PCB.
- A 4-layer board was chosen because it makes the ESD/EFT tests easier to pass and keeps the clock and ADC signals clean. The extra cost over a 2-layer board is small.
