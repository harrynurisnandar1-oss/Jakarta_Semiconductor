Lesson Learned

According to the SPLN standard, a 3-phase metering system includes the following:

Display screen --> Shows measurement results on the LCD.

Historical data --> Stores measurement results from previous periods.

Load profile --> Records measurement results periodically based on specific time intervals.

Instant profile --> Provides a snapshot of measurement results at the time of reading.

Register naming --> Assigns an identifier to each quantity being stored or accessed.

Thus, a single quantity, for instance, active energy can be utilized across multiple categories simultaneously.

Examples:

- Current active energy is shown on the LCD.

- Active energy from the previous month is stored as historical data.

- Active energy is recorded every 15 minutes as a load profile.

- Active energy at the time of a reading is included in the instant profile.

- Imported active energy is assigned its own unique register identifier. EoB stands for "End of Billing," which refers to the closing time of the electricity billing period.
