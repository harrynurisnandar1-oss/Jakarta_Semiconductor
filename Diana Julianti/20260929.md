BOM (Bill of Materials)

A BOM is a complete list of materials, components, and quantities required to manufacture an electronic or mechanical product; meanwhile, reading a schematic refers to the ability to interpret circuit diagrams—which utilize standard symbols and letter codes—in order to assemble or repair a device.
Key BOM components: (Part number, Component name and description, Quantity, Component value and specifications, Manufacturer name and part number, Supplier, Price). https://radicasoftware.com/id/blog/the-importance-of-electrical-bom-how-to-generate-bom-report


Each symbol has a small label next to it:
Reference (Ref): a unique identifier on the board, e.g., R1, C5, U1.
Value: e.g., 10k or 100nF.
Footprint: the physical form mounted on the PCB, e.g., 0603 or LQFP100.
