Lessons Learned

Understanding the operating principle of a Current Transformer (CT), which utilizes electromagnetic induction to convert a high primary current into a smaller, proportional secondary current suitable for measurement. Understanding the use of an ADC to read analog signals and recognizing that ADC readings can fluctuate—even when the potentiometer position remains constant—due to noise from voltage sources, circuitry, wiring, and electromagnetic waves. Recognizing that the sampling of two analog inputs and the calculation of the mean product constitute a form of interpretation in energy measurement. Understand the fundamentals of the C language, including variables, constants, arithmetic operations, control flow, and functions for performing specific tasks.
