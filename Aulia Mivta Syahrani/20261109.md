Lesson Learned

Tariffs involve categorizing electrical energy consumption based on the time of usage (Time-of-Use/ToU).

WBP (Peak Load Period): the period when the system's electricity demand or load reaches its peak.

LWBP (Off-Peak Period): the period outside of the peak load hours.

LWBP2 (Secondary Off-Peak Period): a secondary category for periods outside of the peak load hours.


Naming scheme for register quantities:

Active energy exported (tariff 1) = Phase R active energy exported (tariff 1) + Phase S active energy exported (tariff 1) + Phase T active energy exported (tariff 1)

Active energy exported (tariff 2) = Phase R active energy exported (tariff 2) + Phase S active energy exported (tariff 2) + Phase T active energy exported (tariff 2)

Active energy exported (tariff 3) = Phase R active energy exported (tariff 3) + Phase S active energy exported (tariff 3) + Phase T active energy exported (tariff 3)


Phase R active energy exported = Phase R active energy exported (tariff 1) + Phase R active energy exported (tariff 2) + Phase R active energy exported (tariff 3)

Phase S active energy exported = Phase S active energy exported (tariff 1) + Phase S active energy exported (tariff 2) + Phase S active energy exported (tariff 3)

Phase T active energy exported = Phase T active energy exported (tariff 1) + Phase T active energy exported (tariff 2) + Phase T active energy exported (tariff 3)


Total active energy exported = Phase R active energy exported + Phase S active energy exported + Phase T active energy exported

Total active energy imported = Phase R active energy imported + Phase S active energy imported + Phase T active energy imported
