Lesson Learned

A 3-phase reference meter serves as a benchmark for comparing the measurement results of the smart meter under test.

A precision digital power analyzer is used to measure the power consumed by the smart meter device itself.

It is noted that the Yokogawa WT3000E is capable of measuring harmonics up to the 100th order in specific measurement modes.
Thus, the Yokogawa WT3000E precision digital power analyzer can serve as a reference instrument for the precise measurement of power and electrical parameters. However, upon further verification, the capability to measure harmonics up to the 100th order does not guarantee that the WT3000E's accuracy meets all the harmonic and THD testing requirements specified for the smart meter.

FRAM can be used to store metering data, load profiles, event logs, as well as configuration and calibration data. However, these storage functions can also be fulfilled by Serial NOR Flash memory.

In PCB design, 0-ohm links function as circuit path connectors that can be used for configuration, path isolation during testing, and simplifying the debugging process.
