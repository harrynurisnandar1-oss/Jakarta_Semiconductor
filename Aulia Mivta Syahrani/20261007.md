Lesson Learned



Class 0.5S active energy meters have an error limit of ±0.5% at full load, which can increase to ±1.0% at low current and low power factor.


Class 0.2S active energy meters have stricter error limits, ranging from approximately ±0.2% to ±0.5%, thereby requiring a higher level of measurement precision.



Understand the differences in characteristics and features between the ADE9000 and ADE9078.



Recognize that the ADE9000 is better suited to the specifications of the planned 3-phase smart meter project, particularly regarding the requirements for 128 samples per cycle and harmonic analysis up to the 50th order.





Obstacle Encountered



Further study is required regarding the functions and interrelationships of the ADC, PGA, and DSP, as well as the signal processing operations within the ADE9000, to meet the measurement requirements of three-phase smart meters.
