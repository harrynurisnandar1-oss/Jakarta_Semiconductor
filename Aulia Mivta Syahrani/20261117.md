Lesson Learned

The Point of Common Coupling (PCC) is the connection point between the electric power utility system and a customer's installation specifically, the point on the electrical network where a customer's installation connects to the distribution system, and where other customers may also be connected.

Total Demand Distortion (TDD) is a measure of current harmonic distortion, calculated as the ratio of the total harmonic current components to the maximum load current.
