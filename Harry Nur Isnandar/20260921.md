Peripheral initialization, system clock reconfiguration, and provision of the Tickless Idle hook function di Main.c
- `PreSleepProcessing()`: Called before the CPU enters sleep mode to execute the instruction to enter **Stop 1 Mode**.
- `PostSleepProcessing()`: Called immediately after the CPU wakes up to restore the `SystemClock_Config()` configuration.
