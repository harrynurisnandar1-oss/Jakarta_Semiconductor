Key Points from Remarks:
The speakers emphasized the importance of semiconductor training to enhance Indonesia’s capability in independently mastering chip technology and reducing reliance on imported chips. Semiconductor development encompasses three primary stages: design, fabrication (foundry), and assembly, testing, and packaging (ATP). Through Arm technology, participants are introduced to chip design—a critical aspect of the semiconductor industry. The training is conducted in-person over 3 days, followed by 200 hours of independent online training. This program aims to enhance participants' competencies, enabling them to understand and develop semiconductor technology, so that Indonesia may transition from being merely a user to becoming a creator of such technology.

Execution state & exception model:
•	Armv8-A and Armv9-A support two execution states: AArch32 (32-bit) and AArch64 (64-bit)
•	32-bit wide (R0-R15) registers (backward compatible with Armv7-A)
•	64-bit wide (X0-X30, W0-W30) registers (code can only use the 64)
•	An application may mix A32 and T32 code, but not 32-bit and 64-bit code
•	Processor (PE) can only change execution state at reset or exception level changes.
