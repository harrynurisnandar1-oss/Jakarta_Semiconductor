## 3. Comments, Questions, Complaints
- **Comments:**
  - I2C uses open-drain outputs: devices can only pull SDA/SCL LOW and never drive them HIGH, so pull-up resistors to 3.3 V are required for the lines to return HIGH when released.
  - A TVS (Transient Voltage Suppressor) is a special diode that clamps short voltage spikes, protecting the circuit from surges or disturbances coming through cables.
  - A 0 Ω resistor works as a selectable "assembly switch": R18 and the power mux U5 must never be fitted at the same time.
