## Istilah - istilah yang akan dipelajari

Encrypt: Scrambling data to make it unrecognizable

Decrypt: Unscrambling data to its original format

Cipher: Another word for algorithm

Key: A complex sequence of alpha-numeric characters, produced by the algorithm, that allows you to scramble and unscramble data

Plaintext: Decrypted or unencrypted data (it doesn’t have to be text only)

Ciphertext: Data that has been encrypted

## Ciphers
A cipher is an early or fundamental form of an encryption algorithm.
Purpose & Function: Ciphers are used to conceal, scramble, and transform data so that secret messages cannot be read by unauthorized parties.
### Types of ciphers

1. Concealment ciphers have been used for centuries to hide a message in plain sight.
2. Substitution ciphers requests the replacement of a single letter or character with another based on a specific pattern. The original character is changed into a new one.
3. Transposition Cipher alters the order or position of characters within a message without replacing the letters with other characters. The letters of the original message remain the same, but their arrangement is scrambled (like an anagram), rendering the text unreadable until it is rearranged into its original order.

### Hash
A hash is what is referred to as a one-way function — you use a hash to encrypt something, but the result is never decrypted.
