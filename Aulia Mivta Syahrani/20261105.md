Lesson Learned

It turns out that the detection of voltage dip and swell conditions in the ADE9000 can be handled using the IRQ1 pin. Consequently, the CF4 pin can be configured exclusively for the DREADY function, outputting a "Data Ready" signal to indicate that the ADC conversion data is available and ready to be read by the microcontroller.

"ctest --output-on-failure": runs the CMake testing system and provides details in the event of a failure.
