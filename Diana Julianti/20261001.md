activity description:
Completed the Bill of Materials (BOM) for the 3-Phase Smart Meter, including columns for Quantity, Reference Designator, Description, Value, Tolerance, Voltage, Part Number, and sourcing information for all required components (ADE9000, STM32U575VGT6, nanocrystalline CT, relay, LCD, RTC chip, modem chip, LDO, DC-DC SMPS chip, Hall sensor, supercapacitor charging chip, LCD controller, flash memory, AC-DC chip, and accelerometer). Specifically for the LCD controller, the Holtek HT1621B (SSOP-48-300mil package) was selected based on distributor stock availability.
