Use of Zero Crossing

|**Smart meter requirements**|**Role of zero crossing**|
|-|-|
|Frequency measurement|Determines the period of the electrical waveform|
|Phase sequence check|Helps identify incorrect phase sequences|
|Phase angle measurement|Supports determining the phase relationship between voltage and current signals|
|Energy measurement|Supports cycle-based energy accumulation mechanisms.|

In 3-phase smart meters, zero-crossing serves as a supporting feature for obtaining information on waveform timing, period/frequency, and specific phase conditions. While this feature is available in the ADE9000, it does not require specific processing by the STM32 if the system requirements are limited to reading basic metrology measurement results.

According to official documentation, the ZERA MT3000 supports harmonic measurement up to the 40th order, whereas the smart meter specifications require harmonic analysis up to the 50th order. Consequently, the ZERA MT3000 does not yet meet these specifications.

The WT3000E is a viable candidate for use as a reference instrument for calibrating ADE9000-based 3-phase smart meters. However, it is essential to verify that the input configuration, voltage and current measurement ranges, and the device's accuracy and uncertainty levels align with the smart meter specifications and testing requirements.
