Lessons Learned

Learned that the formulas defined in SPLN serve as the fundamental basis for metrological measurements, while the ADE9000 executes these measurement algorithms digitally via an ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) and a DSP (Digital Signal Processor).

Understood that parameters such as RMS voltage, RMS current, active power, reactive power, apparent power, power factor, phase angle, and THD (Total Harmonic Distortion) have built-in measurement mechanisms within the ADE9000.

Meanwhile, other parameters—such as line-to-line RMS voltage, total energy, and total power—can be derived through additional processing based on the acquired measurement data.

Active power: the actual energy required for a device to function; it powers components like lights, motors, and machinery.

Reactive power: does not perform actual work, but is necessary to maintain magnetic and electric fields.

Apparent power: the combination of both; it represents the total demand placed on the power source.

Signal processing flow as shown in the "Filter-Based Total RMS" diagram in the SPLN (Figure 61):

1. Signal input

xV_PCF or xI_PCF, representing voltage or current samples.

2. Squaring

Indicated by the x² symbol.

3. Filtering

LPF2 processes the squared result to obtain the mean-square component.

4. Offset Correction

Performed using xRMOSOS and a factor of 2^(15).

5. Square Rooting

The square root operation yields the RMS value.

6. Output

The RMS value is stored in the xRMS register.
