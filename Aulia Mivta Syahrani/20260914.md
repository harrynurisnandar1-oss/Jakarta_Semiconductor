Lesson Learned

Understanding the factors that affect ADC reading accuracy, such as potentiometer tolerance and noise.

Understanding that UART is used to transmit ADC readings from the microcontroller to a computer via `printf()` and `HAL_UART_Transmit()`, allowing the data to be displayed in Docklight; meanwhile, a timer serves as the ADC trigger with a 1 kHz sampling rate, and DMA is used to automatically transfer ADC conversion results to a buffer.

Understanding the role of the circular DMA mode and the "half-complete" and "full-complete" callbacks in signaling that the buffer is partially or fully filled and ready for processing.
Understanding that the RMS value is calculated from a set of ADC samples by squaring each sample, calculating the mean of the squared values, and then taking the square root of the result.

Understanding that basic energy measurement can be performed using two ADC channels and calculating the average of the products of the sample pairs.
