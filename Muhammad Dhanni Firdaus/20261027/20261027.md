- about the work sequence of InnoSwitch3-RP:
  (1) When there is AC/DC voltage on the primary side, an internal current source will charge the capacitor at BPP pin through DRAIN pin. Then, the primary controller will start swtiching independently at initial frequency of 25 kHz without waiting for feedback signal
  (2) The energy transfered to the secondary side will start to charge the capacitor at BPS pin through FWD pin or VOUT pin.
  (3) After the secondary controller is activated, it will start to send handshake pulses through FluxLink to the primary controller.
  (4) Once the pulses are received and validated by primary controller, it will stop the independent switching and start to hand over the control to secondary controller.
  (5) The output is monitored live from a resistive divider network by FB pin with and internal reference voltage of 1.265V.
