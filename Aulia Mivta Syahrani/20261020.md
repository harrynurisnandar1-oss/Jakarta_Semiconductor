Activity Description



07:20 20/10/2026 Departed for Graha ITS to attend the Semiconductor Training with Arm.



07:50 20/10/2026 Arrived at Graha ITS.



09:00 20/10/2026 The event begins.



The session began with opening remarks by Dwi Susanto, Ph.D., Managing Director of Technology Advancement at Danantara Indonesia.



This was followed by opening remarks from Rifqi Ansari, S.TP., M.T., MBA, Director of the Electronics and Telematics Industry.



A presentation was delivered by Heri Kuswantoro, Director of Research and Development Talent Development at the Directorate General of Higher Education, Science, and Technology (Ditjen Diktisaintek).



Remarks were also given by Prof. Dr. (HC) Ir. Bambang Pramujati, S.T., M.Sc.Eng., Ph.D., IPU, ASEAN.Eng., Rector of ITS.



Participants listened to and took notes on the "Introduction to Arm" session, presented by Matt Rushton, Director of Arm Training Services.



Next, participants engaged with the "Introduction to Arm CPU – Cortex-A" session, presented by Yun Yi, an Application Engineer at Arm.



Following a break, the session continued with an introduction to "Arm CPU – Cortex-R and Cortex-M," presented by Tim Menasvota, FAE Director.



This was followed by a presentation on "Introduction to Arm Mali: Mobile GPU Architecture and Graphics Fundamentals" by Heyden Lim, as well as a session titled "What Arm Technology Enables."





Lesson Learned



Gained an understanding of the importance of developing the semiconductor industry in Indonesia, particularly in addressing supply constraints for chips and processors—issues that can impact both the availability of components and the cost of electronic devices.



Acquired knowledge regarding Arm CPU architectures, including the functional distinctions between Cortex-A processors (designed for applications and complex operating systems), Cortex-R processors (intended for real-time systems), and Cortex-M processors (widely used in microcontrollers and embedded systems). 



Gaining an understanding of the broad application of Arm technology across various fields—ranging from embedded devices, automotive systems, and mobile devices to other computing systems.
