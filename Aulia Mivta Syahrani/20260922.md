Lessons Learned

Understanding that `defaultTask` is the default task/thread created by STM32CubeMX when FreeRTOS is enabled, which executes the `StartDefaultTask()` function.

Queues serve as a communication medium for sending and receiving data between tasks.

Semaphores act as a synchronization or signaling mechanism between tasks.

Mutexes serve as a mechanism to protect shared resources, preventing simultaneous access by multiple tasks.

The use of `osDelay()` to introduce a delay in a task, and `osThreadYield()` to allow other tasks to execute.
