Lessons Learned

I understand that debugging requires a systematic approach: understanding the system, observing symptoms based on data, narrowing down the source of the problem, changing one thing at a time, and documenting every step of the testing process. I also learned not to overlook the basics and to always verify through testing that the issue has truly been resolved.

Understanding the use of escape sequences in C to represent special characters—such as `\n` for a new line, `\t` for a tab, `\b` for backspace, `\"` for a double quote, `\\` for a backslash, and `/* ... */` for adding comments to the program without affecting the compilation process.

Recognizing that the evolution of the semiconductor industry demands engineers whose competence stems not only from formal education but also from practical experience and a hands-on understanding of the technology.

Understanding format specifiers in `printf()`, such as `%o` for octal, `%x` for hexadecimal, `%c` for a character, `%s` for a string, `%lu` for an unsigned long, and `%%` to display the `%` character itself.

Understanding that FreeRTOS is used to manage various tasks in embedded systems based on timing and priority, ensuring the system responds as required.
