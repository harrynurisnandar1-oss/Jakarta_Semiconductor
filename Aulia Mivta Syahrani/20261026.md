Lesson Learned

Active power is the electrical power used to perform actual work in each phase, measured in watts (W).

Reactive power is the power exchanged between the electrical source and an inductive or capacitive load in each phase, measured in volt-amperes reactive (var).

Active energy is the accumulated usage of active power across all three phases over a specific period, used to calculate electrical energy consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

Reactive energy is the accumulated reactive power in each phase over a specific period, measured in kilovolt-ampere-hours reactive (kvarh).

Apparent power is the product of the effective voltage and current values ​​in each phase, measured in volt-amperes (VA), representing the total capacity of electrical power being supplied.

Power factor is the ratio of active power to apparent power, with an ideal value approaching 1. This parameter indicates the efficiency of electrical power utilization within a 3-phase smart meter system.
