Challenges Encountered
When changing the capacitor footprint from the standard metric version to the `metric_pad` (hand-solder) version, the change must be applied to every capacitor in the design, causing all of their footprints to automatically update to the hand-solder variant. This makes the replacement and subsequent re-verification process quite time-consuming.
